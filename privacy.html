Hune Privacy Policy

Hune is a try-before-the-cut hairstyle preview app. This Privacy Policy explains how Hune handles information on your device when you capture a face photo, adjust length and fringe, compare looks, and save a barber reference card.

Information processed on your device

  • Front-facing photos you take with the camera or select from Photos for hairstyle try-on.
  • Style choices such as hair length, fringe, fit strength, color try options, turn angle, and optional notes.
  • Voice notes you choose to record, which may be converted to text with on-device speech recognition.
  • Saved barber cards, drafts, and local try balances tied to consumable in-app purchases.
  • Purchase transaction identifiers needed to credit packs and chests only once.

Permissions

Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Speech Recognition are requested only when you use the related feature. Hune does not request App Tracking Transparency and does not use advertising identifiers for tracking. Hune does not set a push-notification delegate or send push notifications in this A-side experience.

Storage and retention

Try-on media, drafts, results, and credits are stored in the app sandbox on your device. Credits are stored on this device and cannot be restored after the app is removed or its data is cleared. You may return to the welcome screen with Log Out, or permanently erase local content with Clear All Data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Hune keeps a local ledger of verified transaction identifiers so the same purchase is not credited twice. There is no restore-purchases flow for consumable packs in this app.

Sharing

Hune does not upload your face photos to our servers in this version. If you save a barber card to Photos, the image remains under your device Photos controls.

Children

Hune is intended for general audiences who want a visual haircut reference. Do not submit photos of others without permission.

Contact

For privacy questions about this build, use the Support entry inside the app More screen.
