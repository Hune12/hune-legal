Hune Privacy Policy

Hune is a try-before-the-cut hairstyle preview app. This Privacy Policy explains how Hune handles information on your device when you capture a face photo, adjust length and fringe, compare looks, take voice notes, buy try packs, and save a barber reference card.

Information processed on your device

  • Front-facing photos you take with the camera or select from Photos for hairstyle try-on.
  • Style choices such as hair length, fringe, hairline fit, warm-light correction, and optional text notes.
  • Voice notes you choose to record, which may be converted to text with speech recognition so you can keep length and layer preferences on the look card.
  • Saved barber cards, unfinished drafts, studio history, and local try balances tied to consumable in-app purchases.
  • Verified purchase transaction identifiers needed to credit packs and chests only once.
  • Optional background theme preference you select in More, stored only on this device.

Permissions

Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Speech Recognition are requested only when you use the related feature. Notification permission may be requested the first time you open the Style Shelf so the system can show alerts you allow; Hune does not use a push-notification delegate in this build and does not send remote marketing pushes. Hune does not request App Tracking Transparency and does not use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

How try-on media is used

Your face photo is used locally to compose a hairstyle preview and a barber reference card. Drafts save the current style, length, fringe, fit, and note so you can continue later. When you save a look, Hune stores a composed preview image in the app sandbox and may let you export that image to Photos under your control.

Storage and retention

Try-on media, drafts, results, theme choice, and credits are stored in the app sandbox on your device. Credits are stored on this device and cannot be restored after the app is removed or its data is cleared. You may return to the welcome screen with Log Out, or permanently erase local content with Clear All Data (including drafts, results, purchase ledger entries, and remaining tries).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Hune keeps a local ledger of verified transaction identifiers so the same purchase is not credited twice. Pack and chest prices shown in the shop are the fixed USD catalog prices for this app. There is no restore-purchases flow for consumable packs.

Sharing

Hune does not upload your face photos to our servers in this version. Speech recognition, when used, is handled through Apple’s speech frameworks under Apple’s privacy terms. If you save a barber card to Photos, the image remains under your device Photos controls.

Children

Hune is intended for people who want a visual haircut reference. Do not submit photos of others without permission. Do not use the app to collect images of children without appropriate consent.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when product features change. The in-app Privacy Policy page always shows the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app, use the Support entry inside the More screen.
